Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the two memberships?

Both make you a full member, with a voice and a vote at the General Assembly. Supporting membership also includes the workshops, the digital library and the other benefits, and helps sustain ALKA's projects.

Can I change my membership?

Yes. Write to us at labas@alka.cat and we will change it for the next membership year.

How do I pay and renew?

With a single annual payment by bank transfer (card payment coming soon). We will remind you by email when it is time to renew: there are no automatic charges or direct debits.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. Once we confirm your payment we will send you a receipt for your membership fee.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, at any time, by writing to labas@alka.cat.
