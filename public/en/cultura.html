Area of action

Culture

We create and preserve our heritage

We foster artistic and cultural creation, and safeguard the preservation and transmission of Lithuanian and Catalan cultural heritage. We organize gatherings, exhibitions and projects that bring our traditions to life and open them up to everyone.

Collaborate in this area →

What we do here

Living heritage

We preserve traditions, crafts and language through projects that ease their transmission between generations.

Artistic creation

We support artists and creators, and drive projects that combine art, memory and identity.

Gatherings and celebrations

We organize exhibitions, events and popular festivities that bring Lithuanian and Catalan culture closer to the community.
